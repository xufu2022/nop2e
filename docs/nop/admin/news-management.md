# News Management
role: admin
url: /Admin/News

- Admin can view the news article list — list page loads with heading visible
- Admin can edit a news article title — change saved, updated title visible in news list
- Admin can edit a news article body — rich text content saved on article detail page
- Admin can publish a news article — article marked as published in the list
- Admin can unpublish a news article — article marked as not published in the list
- Admin can set a news article start and end publication date — dates saved on article detail
- Admin can toggle comments on a news article — comments enabled setting saved on detail page
